Quantum Observation over Finite Substrate

We develop a quantum observational layer over a finite relational arithmetic substrate: a Subject and an Object are phase-coherent cyclic subsystems of a common finite Carrier, and observation is the quotient of the Object’s phase cycle by their shared core. Pure states are windings of the Object cycle; measurement vectors are transported core characters, a forced orthogonal basis whose completeness, selection rules, and update are finite character theory rather than postulates. Probability is a coincidence count in the Subject’s cyclotomic ledger: the Born weighting rests on one declared identification, registration data are sample pairs, plus counting theorems within the tally window, with the pair form the unique channel-selective ray of registration functionals on the stationary quarter-turn sector, the minimal degree itself forced.
