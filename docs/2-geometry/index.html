Geometry and Constants in Finite Ring Continuum

The current paper studies the global shell layer of the Finite Ring Continuum framework in the symmetry-complete regime realized here by framed finite fields, \(\Fp(\chron;0,1,\gen)\), with \(\p=4\kap+1\). We show that a single symmetry-complete shell carries a unified finite Euclidean datum for which its continuum comparison interpretation reproduces the familiar structural roles of \(e\), \(\pi\), and \(i\): the phase generator \(\gen\) supplies the one-phase step and the exponential kernel, the shell determines the half-period \(\pi=2\kap\) and the oriented quarter-turn \(\It=-\gen^{\kap}=\gen^{-\kap}\), and the exponential unit \(\eu=\gen^{\It}\) carries the structural role of Euler’s number. In the same shell, the orbital geometry is generated by additive meridian action and multiplicative phase action from that same frame datum.
