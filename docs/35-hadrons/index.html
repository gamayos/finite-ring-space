Ground-State Light Hadron Spectroscopy over Finite Substrate

The light hadron spectrum is reconstructed over a finite relational arithmetic substrate, on a single $\Om$-hard scale. A baryon is the colour-neutral invariant of the triality frame, the determinant residue $\Lambda^3=\mathbf1$ (baryon number), forced by the gluon residue $0$ and completing the Carrier-residue series photon $2$, gluon $0$, hadron $1$. Its constituents are the wrapped composites of confinement, so each mass is $M_B=\sig\,[\,f_{\mathrm{fl}}+\kappa_{\mathrm{hf}}\,g_{\mathrm{spin}}\,]+\Delta^{\mathrm{EM}}$, the confinement scale $\sig\sim\LQCD$ times dimensionless structure, and the scale cancels in every flavour and spin relation.
