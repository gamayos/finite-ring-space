Ground-State Light Hadron Spectroscopy over Finite Substrate

The light hadron spectrum is reconstructed over a finite holographic substrate, on a single $\Om$-hard scale $\sig$. A baryon is the determinant line $\Lambda^3\mathbf3$ of the colour triplet. Among the exterior powers of the triplet it is the one line, beside the vacuum, that the unitary colour frame $\SU(3,\F_2)$ maps to itself.
