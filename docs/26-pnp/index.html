P versus NP: Computation as Counting

We develop $\PC$ versus $\NPC$ as the feasibility instance of the horizon principle over a finite relational substrate. The algebraic carrier is complete as a totality, yet a bounded internal observer has a finite comprehension horizon. The incompleteness migrates from the undecidability of truth to that horizon inaccessibility.
