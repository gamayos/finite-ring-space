Relativistic Algebra over Finite Ring Continuum

We present a formal reconstruction of the conventional number systems, including integers, rationals and complex numbers, as relational constructs over a finite field \( \Fp \). Rather than assuming actual infinity, we define arithmetic and algebra as observer-dependent constructions grounded in finite field symmetries. Consequently, we formulate relational analogues of the conventional number classes, expressed relationally with respect to a chosen reference frame.
