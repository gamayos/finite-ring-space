Scale-Shift and Fractional Fourier Transform as Rotations over Finite Holographic Substrate

This paper constructs the fractional Fourier transform exactly on a finite holographic substrate. In the finite ring continuum framework the substrate is a finite totality whose count bounds the label plane of an embedded observer. A shell of capacity \texorpdfstring$\kap$κ is the substrate at one level, presented by the prime field with \texorpdfstring$4\kap+1$4κ+1 elements and a frame.
