Emergent Lorentzian Spacetime over Finite Holographic Substrate

We derive Lorentzian spacetime on a finite holographic substrate: a finite, timeless totality whose capacity bounds the state count of every embedded observer. The observer is a prime shell $\Fp$, $\p=4\kap+1$, embedded in the Carrier $\FOm$ with $\p^{2}<\Om$, and its clock is the drive $x\mapsto\gen x$ of its frame. Three cycles build the geometry: the additive register $C_{\p}$, the drive $C_{\p-1}$, and the norm-one group $C_{\p+1}$ of the temporal plane $-\gen t^{2}+x^{2}$, anisotropic because a generator is a nonsquare.
