Fermion Flavour Sector over Finite Substrate

The flavour sector of the Standard Model is reconstructed over a finite relational arithmetic substrate. The substrate fixes the structural half, one fermion generation as the spinor $\mathbf{16}$, the Higgs mass coupling $m=yv$, and the three-generation count. The paper derives the masses, the mixings, as well as quantitative residue.
