Fermion Flavour Sector over Finite Substrate

The flavour sector of the Standard Model is reconstructed over a finite relational arithmetic substrate. Earlier work on that substrate fixes one fermion generation as the spinor sixteen, the Higgs coupling of mass, and the count of three generations. This paper treats the masses and the mixings.
