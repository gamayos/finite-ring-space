Schrödinger and Dirac Dynamics

The holographic principle bounds the information a bounded observer can register. The finite ring continuum framework takes the bound as the substrate: the observer is a prime shell $\Fp$ with $\p=4\kap+1$, read through a frame of origin, unit and drive, whose capacity $\kap$ counts its quarter-turn packets. We give the operator core of Schrödinger and Dirac dynamics on that shell.
