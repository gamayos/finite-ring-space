Emergent Lorentzian Space-Time over Finite Holographic Substrate

We derive Lorentzian space-time on a finite holographic substrate: a finite, timeless totality whose capacity bounds the state count of every embedded observer. The observer is a prime shell $\Fp$, $\p=4\kap+1$, embedded in the Carrier $\FOm$ with $\p^{2}<\Om$. Its frame is an origin, a unit and a drive $x\mapsto\gen x$ with $\gen$ a generator of $\Fpx$, and the drive is its clock.
