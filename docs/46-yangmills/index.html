Yang–Mills and the Mass Gap over Finite Holographic Substrate

We restate the operational content of Yang–Mills theory on a finite holographic substrate: a finite, timeless totality, the Carrier, whose capacity bounds the state count of every embedded observer. The observer, a Subject, is a prime shell $\Fp$, and it reads colour in a unitary frame, $\SU(3)$ over its quadratic extension. We prove three main results on the finite frame.
