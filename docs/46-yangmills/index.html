Yang–Mills and the Mass Gap over Finite Holographic Substrate

We restate the operational content of Yang–Mills theory on a finite holographic substrate: a finite, timeless totality, the Carrier, whose capacity bounds the state count of every observer in it. The observer is a prime shell $\Fp$, $\p=4\kap+1$, and the colour frame is $\SU(3)$ over the Subject’s quadratic extension. A registration of a state is a line of its space that every element of its frame maps to itself; the residue of the state is the number of such lines.
