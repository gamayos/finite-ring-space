Euclidean–Lorentzian Dichotomy and Algebraic Causality in Finite Ring Continuum

We show that a construction formed by a pair of finite rings: a prime fields $\Fp$ (Subject) embedded in a torsor $\F_\Om$ (Carrier), with $\p^{2}<\Om$, exhibits a Euclidean–Lorentzian dichotomy. A diagonal quadratic form in four variables is Lorentzian when its time coefficient is in the nonsquare class of $\Fpx$. The time coefficient of the Subject is its drive $\g$, which is a nonsquare.
