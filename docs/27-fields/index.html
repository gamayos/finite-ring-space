Standard-Model Interactions over Finite Substrate

The electromagnetic, weak and strong interactions and one generation of matter are stated on a finite holographic substrate: a finite, timeless totality, the Carrier, whose capacity bounds the state count of every embedded observer. The observer, a Subject, is a prime shell $\Fp$ with $\p=4\kap+1$ and a frame of an origin, a unit and a drive. A change of frame that varies from cell to cell is a gauge symmetry, and the connection that compensates it is the field.
