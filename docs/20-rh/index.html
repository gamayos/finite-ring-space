Riemann Hypothesis over the Holographic Substrate

We construct a finite-field dictionary for the Riemann zeta function on the critical line and read the Riemann Hypothesis through it. On a prime shell $\F_p$, $p=4\kappa+1$, with primitive root $g$ and the quadratic extension $\F_p(\eta)$, the half-turn $2^{-1}=2\kappa+1=-\pi$ is at once the centre of the self-dual line $\mathrm{Tr}\,z=1$ and the fixed point of the functional-equation involution $z\mapsto1-z$, and de-frames to the critical real part $\tfrac12$; the scale-shift $x\mapsto gx$ is a unitary permutation with the characters of the cycle as eigenvectors, eigenphases $2\pi j/(p-1)$; the finite zeta vanishes on every nonterminal slot. The finite-field statements are theorems for every shell, proved in Lean 4.
