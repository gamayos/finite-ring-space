Riemann Hypothesis over Finite Holographic Substrate

We read the Riemann Hypothesis over a finite holographic substrate: a finite totality whose capacity, the de Sitter entropy, bounds the state count of every embedded observer, coordinatized by a frame as a prime field and read through the embedded Subject fields whose label space fits the count. On every such field the half-turn is the centre of the self-dual line in the quadratic extension, the fixed locus of the functional-equation involution and the image of the critical real part, and the scale-shift is a unitary permutation whose eigenvectors are the characters of the cycle: theorems for every field, proved in Lean 4 without axioms. A Subject reads zeta on the line to the height of its own cycle from the residues below its horizon, where its arithmetic coincides with the Carrier’s.
