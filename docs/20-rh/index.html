Riemann Hypothesis over Finite Holographic Substrate

A shell is a prime field with $\p=4\kp+1$ residues, read by an observer through a frame. A prime is a label of the observer’s window, up to the observational horizon $\q$ with $\q^{2}<\p$. The modes of the shell are the powers $x^{k}$, and its zeta is the sum of a mode over the nonzero residues.
