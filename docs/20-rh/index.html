Riemann Hypothesis over Finite Holographic Substrate

A shell is a prime field with $\p=4\kp+1$ residues, read by an observer through a frame. Its residues are the only complete list on it, and the meridians carry all of them and the latitudes the nonzero residues, each in a different role. A prime is a label on the real line, inside the observer’s window below $\sqrt\p$.
